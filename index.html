COURIER BRIEFING

Five things. Then the city is yours.

  • 1Move between lanes. Swipe left/right or use ◀ ▶ buttons. Arrow keys or A / D on desktop.
  • 2Jump over barriers. Swipe up, tap ▲, or press SPACE / ↑ / W.
  • 3Slide under lasers. Swipe down, tap ▼, or press ↓ / S.
  • 4Collect energy crystals. Every crystal adds score and feeds your combo.
  • 5Avoid everything else. Drones, walls and electrical hazards must be dodged with lane changes.
